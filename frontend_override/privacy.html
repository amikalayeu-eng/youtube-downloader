Privacy

This downloader is designed to minimize stored data. It does not require an account and does not build a permanent history of downloaded YouTube URLs.

Temporary job data

A YouTube URL and basic metadata are stored only as needed to analyze and process the active job. Redis job metadata expires automatically. Finished files are temporary and are deleted after their configured TTL.

Abuse protection

The service uses an anonymous browser session identifier plus a one-way salted hash of network address information for rate limits and concurrent-job controls. Raw IP addresses are not written into application job metadata by the application.

Analytics

Built-in counters record visits, analyze actions, download starts, successes, failures, selected formats and video-quality choices. The analytics events do not include the YouTube URL.

Cookies

One first-party, HTTP-only session cookie is used for anonymous job ownership and abuse protection. It is not an advertising cookie.
